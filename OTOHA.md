# Otoha AI port

This fork carries an unofficial port of dsh-context (bowenliang123/dsh-context) to [Otoha AI](https://otohaai.com), published on the Otoha skill market (https://skills.otohaai.com) as `market:baryon/dsh-context`: a read-only page with statistics about the current conversation's context.

- The port is the `otoha/` folder: exactly the package the market publishes, with its full source, the built page, the upstream licence (Apache-2.0), `NOTICE.md` (source, author, pinned commit `2bde34d`, what was changed and why) and `MODIFICATIONS.md` (how the package was made, with checksums).
- Everything outside `otoha/` is upstream. This is not a release of, or endorsed by, the original author.
